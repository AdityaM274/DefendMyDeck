DefendMyDeck: follow one claim

Pitch → editable claim → supplied evidence → consistency audit → rehearsal → comparison → export.

  1. public/index.html defines the three workspace screens.
  2. public/app.js handles forms, links sources to claims and keeps state. Saving is optional; imports are validated.
  3. public/core.js → extractClaims identifies candidate assertions with text patterns. Users correct misses manually.
  4. auditClaim compares numeric anchors and checks evidence type; it does not establish truth.
  5. questionFor creates a template question from the exact claim and source gap.
  6. evaluateAnswer reports source, measurement and limitation mentions. Human judgment is still required.
  7. contextKey invalidates old attempts after evidence or claim changes. validateWorkspace rebuilds imported data instead of trusting stored feedback.
  8. server.mjs serves only allowlisted assets on loopback. tests/ checks behavior and server boundaries.
